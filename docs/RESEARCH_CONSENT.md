# Research Consent — Hishab User Interviews & In-App Feedback

Status: **User interviews pending — no qualitative claims are made yet.**

## What this study is

Hishab is a hackathon project studying one outcome: reducing the rate of
user-months with a cash-flow shortfall (completed months where recorded
expenses exceed recorded income). We are interviewing Bangladesh MFS users
about tight months, money habits, language preference, and what app help
would be most useful.

## Voluntary participation

- Participation is fully voluntary. You may skip any question, pause, or
  withdraw at any time with no penalty and no effect on any service.
- You must be 18 or older to participate.

## What we collect

- Interview: anonymised notes/audio (with your permission), participant code
  (e.g. P01), language comfort, feature ranking, short general themes.
- In-app (optional, separate checkbox): useful / not-useful vote, which part
  helped most (forecast / expense explanation / wording / suggested action /
  none), optional free text, interface language.
- We do NOT collect names, phone numbers, NID, wallet numbers, passwords,
  or raw transaction lists for research. In-app feedback stores
  privacy-safe aggregates only.

## How data is used

- Only aggregated, anonymised findings are reported (counts, distributions,
  themes). No identifiable quotes without extra explicit permission.
- Consent records are stored separately from notes.
- You may ask us to delete your notes/feedback at any time before
  aggregation by contacting the team with your participant code.

## Risks & benefits

- Minimal risk: recalling tight-money periods may be uncomfortable.
- No payment is promised. There is no guarantee the app will improve your
  finances. Findings are observational, not medical/financial advice.
